Welcome to the Farm

For more than three generations, Hollow Creek Farm has welcomed families to slow down, explore the fields, and enjoy the best of each season.

Whether you're picking sunflowers in May, navigating the corn maze in October, or shopping the Holiday Market in December — there's always something waiting for you here.

Year-Round

Something for Every Season

Spring

Bloom & Grow

Sunflower u-pick, strawberry picking, baby animal visits, and fresh seedlings for your garden.

Summer

Harvest & Savor

Blueberry picking, farm-fresh produce, and ticketed farm-to-table dinners in the pavilion.

Fall

Maze & Harvest

5-acre corn maze, pumpkin patch, hayrides, fresh cider, and the Fall Harvest Festival.

Winter

Market & Cheer

Holiday Market, Christmas trees, wreaths, and the Farm Store open year-round.

Open Year-Round

The Farm Store

Located inside our restored 1890s barn, the Hollow Creek Farm Store is stocked with fresh eggs, raw honey, seasonal jams, baked goods, fresh cider, and farm merchandise.
