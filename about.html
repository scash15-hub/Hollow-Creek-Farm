Our Story

Three generations of family, farming, and welcoming visitors home.

Where It All Began

Bill & Margaret Calloway, 1962

What started as a small vegetable operation on 140 acres has grown into one of the region's most beloved agritainment destinations. Bill and Margaret Calloway planted the first seeds — not just in the fields, but in a tradition of welcoming people to slow down and enjoy the land.

From the very beginning, Hollow Creek Farm was about more than growing food. It was about building something that would last — a place where families could connect with the seasons and with each other.

The Journey

64 Years in the Making

1962

The Farm Begins

Bill and Margaret Calloway establish Hollow Creek Farm as a small family vegetable operation on 140 acres.

1980s

Growing the Land

The farm expands with a small orchard, sunflower fields, and the restored 1890s barn that still stands today.

2000s

The Corn Maze Arrives

The fall corn maze opens for the first time, drawing crowds from across the region and making fall at Hollow Creek a tradition.

2008

A New Generation

Dana and Rob Calloway take over the farm, bringing fresh energy and expanding seasonal events and the farm store.

Today

The Family Grows

Tyler, Meg, and Sam — Dana and Rob's kids — grew up on the farm and now help run it full time alongside their parents.

The Calloways

Still Family. Still Farming.

Dana and Rob took over from Bill and Margaret in 2008, and their three kids — Tyler, Meg, and Sam — grew up running through these same fields. Today they all work the farm together, side by side.

Visitors often describe Hollow Creek Farm as the kind of place that makes you slow down. That's exactly what the Calloways set out to create — and exactly what they'll keep building for the next generation.
